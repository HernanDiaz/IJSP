\section*{Description}

This document reports per-instance RE\,(\%) and median runtime for all 70 Taillard
Interval Job Shop Scheduling Problem (IJSP) instances, comparing four solvers:
$ABC_{E3}$ \cite{DiazFEABC2023}, $ESABC$ \cite{DiazICAE2023},
$TS\text{-}N_2$ (this work), and $TS\text{-}N_8$ (this work).

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Metric.}
$\mathrm{RE}(\%) = (E[\mathbf{C}_{\max}] - \mathrm{LB})\,/\,\mathrm{LB} \times 100$,
where $\mathrm{LB}$ is the published deterministic lower bound \cite{Taillard1993}.
A negative RE\,(\%) indicates that the interval midpoint lies below the
deterministic lower bound; this can occur because the interval and deterministic
problems are structurally distinct (the interval midpoint is not bounded below
by the deterministic lower bound).

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Columns.} For each solver: \emph{Best} = best RE\,(\%) over 30 runs;
\emph{Avg} = mean RE\,(\%) over 30 runs (bold = row minimum);
$t$ = median runtime in seconds (TS) or average runtime in seconds (ABC methods).

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Instances.} The 70 Taillard IJSP instances (TA1--TA70) are derived from
the classical Taillard JSP benchmarks \cite{Taillard1993} by applying a
$\pm7.5\%$ symmetric interval perturbation to each nominal processing time:
$\mathbf{p}_{ij} = [0.925\,p_{ij}^{*},\, 1.075\,p_{ij}^{*}]$.
Seven size classes are covered: $15{\times}15$ (TA1--10),
$20{\times}15$ (TA11--20), $20{\times}20$ (TA21--30),
$30{\times}15$ (TA31--40), $30{\times}20$ (TA41--50),
$50{\times}15$ (TA51--60), $50{\times}20$ (TA61--70).

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{TS configuration.} Irace-tuned tabu search (Phase~B of the main paper),
30 independent runs per instance, 15-minute wall-clock time limit per run,
on an Intel Xeon E5-2680\,v4 at 2.40\,GHz (WSL2 Linux).

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Irace training instances.} The irace tuning of Phase~B (Section~7.1
of the main paper) used a stratified random subset of 20 of the 82 benchmark
instances ($\approx$\,24\%), drawn uniformly at random within each size
stratum. The 62 instances \emph{not} included in the list below were held
out from tuning and contribute the unbiased evaluation reported in the
main paper and in Tables~\ref{tab:supp_taillard_i}--\ref{tab:supp_taillard_ii}.

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item \emph{Classical} (3 of 12): \texttt{ft10}, \texttt{abz7}, \texttt{la27}.
  \item \emph{Taillard $15{\times}15$} (2 of 10): TA3, TA8.
  \item \emph{Taillard $20{\times}15$} (2 of 10): TA12, TA17.
  \item \emph{Taillard $20{\times}20$} (3 of 10): TA21, TA24, TA29.
  \item \emph{Taillard $30{\times}15$} (2 of 10): TA32, TA36.
  \item \emph{Taillard $30{\times}20$} (3 of 10): TA43, TA47, TA50.
  \item \emph{Taillard $50{\times}15$} (2 of 10): TA52, TA57.
  \item \emph{Taillard $50{\times}20$} (3 of 10): TA63, TA67, TA70.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The full irace input files (\texttt{instances.txt}, \texttt{parameters.txt},
\texttt{scenario.txt}) and the resulting \texttt{iracedump.rda} files for
each of the five neighbourhoods are archived at~\cite{DiazData2026}.
