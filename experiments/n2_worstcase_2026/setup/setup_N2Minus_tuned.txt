# setup_N2_tuned.txt EXCEPT localsearch.neighbourhood = ijsp.makespan.n2minus.

# bound (G-, comp=1) only. It is the mirror of N2Plus and serves as the
# control: under LEX2 (upper-bound-first) it is expected to underperform,
# confirming the effect is specific to the worst case, not to using a single
# component. Hyperparameters are the irace-tuned N2 config (NOT re-tuned).
